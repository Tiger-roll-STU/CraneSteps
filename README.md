## 简体中文

### 这是什么游戏？

小梅教火闪电练舞，你负责按准落下的音符。接住短按、长按和双押，在宣纸与水墨的山间舞台上，跟着《活侠传》的音乐一步步练成。

- **三章、三档难度：** 从普通起步，逐曲解锁困难与小梅地狱；章节内可选择曲目挑战。
- **自由练习：** 保留判定、连击和结算统计，不扣气、不计入正式成绩，适合慢慢熟悉已解锁的谱面。
- **生存模式：** 一条命连续挑战 39 首，跨曲继承气息；命中回气，失误扣气。可以中途暂存、去练习或关掉游戏，再回来继续。
- **梅花成就：** 通关、全连与全音完美留下不同颜色的梅花，也有值得截图留念的结算。
- **按自己的习惯玩：** 自定义键位、下落速度、音量与时差调整；试听可开启击打音，方便听清谱面。
- **同人特别曲：** 收录独立编谱的《子夜寄战书-虎皮卷REMIX版》，通过完整 OST1 校验后可解锁单曲试听和游玩。

### 下载与开始

1. 打开上方**下载最新版**，在 **Assets** 中下载 `CraneSteps-1.0.0-Windows-x64.zip`。
2. **完整解压**，双击 `仙鹤迷踪步CraneSteps.exe`。保留旁边的 `game` 文件夹，不要在压缩包内直接运行。
3. 首次游玩可以先体验教学。默认按键为 **D / F / J / K**，**Esc** 暂停，**F11** 切换全屏；语言与键位均可在设置中调整。目前为键盘操作，不支持手柄。

GitHub 自动生成的 `Source code (zip)` / `Source code (tar.gz)` 是本说明仓库的归档，**不是游戏安装包**。

### 音乐装载与隐私

为了尊重官方音乐权益，发布包不附带付费官方 OST 歌曲。请支持官方：**[购买 OST1](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2983230/) · [购买 OST2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3968660/)**。

购买、下载后，在游戏的“装载 OST”中**手动指定音乐文件夹**，或将官方原文件复制到 `game/music/OST1`、`game/music/OST2`，再在游戏内装载。

**游戏仅识别官方 OST 文件的音乐指纹。** 不登录 Steam、不验证 Steam 购买记录，也不会自动扫描电脑寻找音乐。只读取游戏自己的音乐目录，以及你主动指定的目录和子目录；不上传音乐文件。请保持官方文件原样。

| 游玩内容 | 音乐要求 |
|---|---|
| 普通单曲 | 有声游玩需对应官方 OST；未装载时可静音游玩 |
| REMIX 特别曲单曲 | 需完整 OST1 校验，不要求 OST2 |
| 生存模式 | 需完整 OST1、OST2 校验；固定 39 首，含 REMIX |

生存还需先通关该难度的全部 38 首开放官方曲目。各难度分别解锁、分别保存暂存和最高总分。完整气息及暂存规则可在生存大厅的**“生存玩法说明”**查看。

### 更新和保留进度

**先退出游戏，再更新。确认新版继承成功前，不要删除旧文件夹。**

- 英文目录版：保留完整 `game/data`，以及自己装载的音乐和自定义资料。可将新版解压覆盖至原目录，或把这些个人目录复制到新目录。
- 早期中文目录版：将旧 `游戏/data` 的内容复制到新 `game/data`；音乐等个人目录按需迁移。**不要复制旧 `runtime`。** OST 地址变化后，在游戏中重新指定位置。
- 正常成绩、设置及兼容的生存暂存可以继承。旧 38 首生存暂存不能接续新版 39 首路线，需重新开始该挑战。
- 存档有基础完整性校验，请不要手动改写。正常暂存和跨电脑迁移保留；旧版或作者版生存续玩会单独标注。校验不等于真人操作证明，也不能完全防作弊。

想移除游戏时，退出后删除解压文件夹即可；**同目录的存档与自装音乐也会一并删除，请先保留需要的内容**。分享给朋友请使用原始发布 ZIP，不要把装载了官方音乐或个人存档的文件夹重新打包。

## Crane Steps

A free, unofficial four-key rhythm fan game inspired by *Legend of Mortal*, created by TigerRollStudio. Play as the crane and join Xiaomei for dance practice in the mountains.

Features include three difficulty levels, remappable keyboard controls, adjustable note speed, free practice, plum-blossom achievement marks, and a survival mode you can suspend and resume. Practice keeps judgement feedback and statistics without draining HP or saving competitive scores.

**[Download the latest release](https://github.com/Tiger-roll-STU/CraneSteps/releases/latest)** and choose the Windows ZIP under **Assets**. Extract the entire archive and run `仙鹤迷踪步CraneSteps.exe`. Keep the adjacent `game` folder. The automatically generated `Source code` archives are not the game. Requires 64-bit Windows; keyboard only. Default keys: **D/F/J/K**. **Esc** pauses; **F11** toggles fullscreen. Select English in Settings.

**Paid official OST songs are not included.** Please purchase and download [OST1](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2983230/) and/or [OST2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3968660/) separately, then select the album folders in the game. You can also copy the original files into `game/music/OST1` and `game/music/OST2` and load them there.

The game recognizes official OST music fingerprints. It does not log into Steam, verify purchase history, or automatically search your computer for music. It reads its own music folders and the folders and subfolders you select. Music files are not uploaded.

Ordinary single-song play is available silently without the corresponding OST. The REMIX special song requires the complete OST1. **Survival requires both complete OST albums** and follows a fixed 39-song route including the REMIX. Clear all 38 available official songs on a difficulty to unlock that difficulty's survival mode.

Survival starts at 100 HP. Hits restore HP, mistakes reduce it, and remaining HP carries into the next song without a refill. You can suspend a run, practise in ordinary play, close the game, and resume later. Each difficulty has its own checkpoint and best score. Full rules are available from the survival lobby.

Before updating, close the game and preserve the entire `game/data` folder, your own music, and any custom assets. For older Chinese-folder packages, copy the contents of `游戏/data` into the new `game/data`; do not copy the old `runtime`. Keep the old folder until you have confirmed your progress transferred. Old 38-song survival checkpoints cannot continue on the new 39-song route. Compatible legacy checkpoints can resume with a separate label. Saves have basic integrity checks; do not edit them manually. These checks are not proof of human play or a guarantee against cheating.

Report issues through [Issues](https://github.com/Tiger-roll-STU/CraneSteps/issues) or the original release post. Include your game version, OS, song, difficulty and timestamp, with a screenshot or clip if helpful. Do not post official music or private files.
